Add a little magic to your screen.

Sparkles displays animated sparkles as a transparent, system-wide overlay. The sparkles appear over other apps without interfering with touch input, so you can keep using your phone normally.

Features:
• System-wide transparent overlay
• Touch-through display
• Five sparkle shapes
• Smooth animations
• Adjustable sparkle count, size, lifetime, and animation speed
• Runs as a foreground service
• No accounts or network connection required

Give your phone some sparkles. ✨
